Privacy Policy — Salat Khair al-Quroon

Android app “Salat Khair al-Quroon” (package org.turath.salat) · Last updated: November 4, 2026

In short

The app is free for everyone, for the sake of Allah. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data. Your settings and your location stay on your phone, except for the small mosque-search requests described below.

Who we are

The app is published by Turath Khair al-Quroon (Abu Hasan Mian Saeed), Ontario, Canada. Website and contact: https://turathkhairalquroon.com

What the app uses, and why

  • Location (approximate or precise): used on your phone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction for where you are. You can also choose a city by hand.
  • Camera: used only for the live Qibla camera view. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications, exact alarms and full-screen alerts: to show prayer-time reminders and play the adhan on time.
  • Vibration, sound and “Do Not Disturb” access: to vibrate before the iqamah and to restore your phone’s previous ringer state afterwards.
  • Foreground services, wake lock, start at boot and battery-optimization exemption: so reminders and the adhan keep working after a restart and while the screen is off.
  • Internet: used only for the mosque finder (see below) and for opening links you tap.

Mosque finder

When you use the mosque finder, the app sends the map area around you (a rectangle of coordinates), or the place name you type, to our website server and to OpenStreetMap-based services (Overpass, Photon and Nominatim) to get the list of mosques. These requests are not linked to your name or to any account. We do not store your location or build any history. Like any web service, these servers may process technical data such as your IP address for a short time to answer the request. If you tap “directions”, the address is opened in your maps app or browser.

Data stored on your phone

Your settings (city, calculation method, adhan choices, iqamah times and similar) are saved on your phone only. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Sharing, children and security

  • We do not sell or share personal data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • The app is intended for adults (18 and over) and does not knowingly collect information from children.
  • Requests to our server and to the services above use HTTPS.

Your choices

You can turn off location, camera or notification permission at any time in your phone’s settings; the related features will then stop working. There is no account or server-side profile to delete.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: please contact us through turathkhairalquroon.com.
