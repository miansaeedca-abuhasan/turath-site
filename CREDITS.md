# صلاۃ خیرالقرون — sources

- Cities, coordinates and time zones: GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org), CC BY 4.0.
- Prayer times: the PrayTimes.org method (sun declination, equation of time, angle-based high-latitude rule).
- Hijri calendar: Umm al-Qura (java.time HijrahDate), with the user's ±2 day correction; the date changes at Maghrib.
- Birdsong 1: "Birds waking #3", BigSoundBank (https://bigsoundbank.com), CC0.
- Adhan recordings: supplied by the owner of Turath Khair al-Quroon.
- Birdsong (Wikimedia Commons, from xeno-canto; cut to 25 s, levelled): Nightingale — Phoenicurus ochruros, XC416395, CC BY-SA 4.0;
  Blackbird — Olivier Grimm, XC561032, CC BY-SA 4.0; Robin — Dan Stowell, XC124862, CC BY-SA 3.0; Skylark — Pascal Christe,
  XC482777, CC BY-SA 4.0; Wren — Borja, XC425718, CC BY-SA 4.0; Dawn chorus — Alexander Wendt, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Short calls: cut from the owner's adhan recordings (tools/cut_calls.py).
